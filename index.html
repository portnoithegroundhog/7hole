Acoustic End Correction Physics

Sound waves extend slightly past the physical opening of a pipe before reflecting. For an un-flanged open tube end or tone hole, the standard acoustic end correction is:

ΔL = 0.6 × R

where R is the internal bore radius (R = Bore Diameter / 2).

Flute Acoustics & Calculation Guide

1. Half-Wavelength Physics

An open cylindrical pipe vibrates at half-wavelength fundamentals ($\lambda / 2$). The uncorrected acoustic wave length $L_0$ for a frequency $f$ and speed of sound $v$ is given by:

L₀ = v / (2 × f)

2. End Correction (0.6R)

The standing wave inside the flute extends slightly beyond the physical opening into surrounding ambient air. To tune the note accurately, this virtual extension $0.6 \times R$ is subtracted:

L_final = L₀ - 0.6R

3. Temperature Impact

Speed of sound in dry air varies with temperature $T$ (°C):

v ≈ 331.3 × √(1 + T / 273.15) m/s

At room temperature (20°C / 68°F), $v \approx 13,504\text{ in/s}$. At warm breath temperature (24.5°C / 76°F), $v \approx 13,622\text{ in/s}$.
